# Claims

One row per claim the argument depends on. The columns: its id; the claim as the paper may word it; what it rests on; *Campaign's verdict*, the verdict of an investigation run elsewhere, for a result pinned from one, otherwise empty; *Paper's verdict*, one of *supportable*, *if narrowed*, *open*, *unsupported* or *withdrawn*, whose meanings are in `skills/paper/evidence.md`; and *Checked by*, who checked the paper's verdict and against what. A long reading goes in a note under `paper/ingest/`, not here.
