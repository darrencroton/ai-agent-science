# Claims

One row per claim the argument depends on: the claim as the paper may word it (no stronger than its test, core rule 4), what it rests on (a path, a key of `refs.bib` or a pinned copy, each openable in one step), and the paper's verdict: supportable as worded, supportable if narrowed (with the narrower wording), or unsupported. A claim whose evidence came from a campaign (an investigation run elsewhere whose findings are pinned here) carries that campaign's verdict beside the paper's. A long reading goes in a note under `paper/ingest/`, not here.
