# Outline

The only authority for the argument. Changed only on the author's decision; a framing set aside is recorded in the journal, not kept here. Three parts, in this order: the intent, the argument spine, and the sections table.

## Intent
What the paper is for, who must be convinced, and what it is not, in the author's own words where they exist, each line dated. When the author changes their mind the line is rewritten and the journal records the change.

_Empty. `start` proposes the first version from the author's answers._

## Argument
The spine: the claims in the order the paper makes them, each pointing at its row in `paper/claims.md`.

_Empty._

## Sections
One row per section: its role (methods, results, discussion or framing: `skills/paper/sections.md`), the budget in words, and what it must carry (claim ids and named items). What a section must carry is never dropped to meet its budget; a conflict goes to the author before the prose is written (core rule 7).
