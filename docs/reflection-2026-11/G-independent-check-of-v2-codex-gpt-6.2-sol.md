## SECTION: STATES

The distinction is sound. Use **sketch**, **draft**, and **checked for a named use**. “Relied on” describes what someone did and can obscure what was actually checked. `docs/reflection-2026-11/REFLECTION.md:123–150`

- **Sketch.** A visibly provisional question, outline, figure sequence or passage. Mark factual assertions with traces or gaps. The scientist can explore alternatives and commission early criticism without promoting the sketch. `skills/_shared/core.md:7–15`; `skills/paper/sections.md:54`
- **Draft.** The writer has opened the sources, recorded traces or gaps, and run applicable mechanical checks. A clean lint result leaves semantic support and missing traces to a reader. `skills/paper/draft.md:17–38`; `tools/traces.py:581–595`
- **Checked for a named use.** Before a factual claim drives a decision or supports another claim, identify the scope, text version, evidence pins and independent check. Seeking exploratory feedback on labelled provisional material requires no blanket check. Mark affected scope unchecked when its wording, evidence or interpretation changes. An unreachable source leaves that claim unchecked, and submission retains its full checks. When dropping a section, account for claims that survive elsewhere and resolve its promises. `skills/_shared/core.md:11–15`; `skills/paper/revise.md:17–30`; `skills/paper/finish.md:3–10`
