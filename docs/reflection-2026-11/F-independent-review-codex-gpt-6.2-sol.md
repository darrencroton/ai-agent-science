## SECTION: PART1_TOP_DOWN_REVIEW

### L1 — Vision against the owner’s purpose

The vision gets the central commitment right. The scientist owns the science, argument and voice. The writing agent makes each checkable statement answerable to a source or exposes the gap. The independent reader tests that support, and the scientist remains responsible for signing. Preserving this division matters more than preserving the current sequence of work. `docs/VISION.md:13`, `docs/VISION.md:27–51`, `.orchestrator/reflection-review-brief.md:3–10`

The vision leaves three parts of the owner’s purpose largely unstated. **Writing alongside evolving science** appears indirectly in the provision for separate investigations, while the non-goal describes writing up work someone has already done. **Finding gaps** appears as a property of honest prose, with little recognition that identifying a missing comparison or a better research question is itself an outcome. **Independent use** is absent from the audience and product descriptions, even though the README explicitly offers readers to investigations. The vision also leaves the priority between results, methods, software and data-release papers unstated. The software parallel reinforces a linear reading by putting the agreed outline and claims before prose. That is a sensible relationship for a drafting pass and a poor description of how an entire investigation develops. `docs/VISION.md:21–43`, `docs/VISION.md:53–73`, `docs/VISION.md:105–109`, `README.md:45`, `.orchestrator/reflection-review-brief.md:5–10`

Keep the vision’s distinction between mechanical checks and judgement, and make the guarantees more precise. The LaTeX macro raises a package error when invoked in final mode, and the documented build handles that error. ADS writes the returned BibTeX text, and trace lint checks citation keys. Neither operation verifies the later contents of a bibliography against the original export. The record can support an author’s decision to share evidence, and the submission procedure keeps traces in the private workspace. The backlog already identifies these wording problems. `docs/VISION.md:73–95`, `docs/BACKLOG.md:15–19`, `skeleton/paper-workspace/manuscript/scipaper.sty:34–43`, `skeleton/paper-workspace/AGENTS.md:42–48`, `tools/ads.py:416–424`, `tools/traces.py:581–595`, `skills/paper/finish.md:9–11`

### L2 — Architecture

The map has the right distinctions. Source fidelity, claim strength, communication and likely referee objections require different inputs and yield different decisions. The single feedback loop is also worth keeping, since a checker’s finding and a co-author’s comment both need a disposition and any resulting change needs verification. Presenting the readers as callable services would make the map easier to enter. The current map gives little direction to a returning scientist who brings a new plot that changes the question, or to a campaign manager who wants help framing an idea before there is a paper. `README.md:18–45`, `skills/paper/SKILL.md:8–36`, `skills/paper/review.md:16–36`, `skills/paper/revise.md:3–31`

“A map, not a pipeline” holds at the level of activities. Inside drafting, reading the field precedes the narrative and hand-offs, every paragraph plan goes to a stylist, and every drafted section goes to the stylist and checker before it is called drafted. The procedures would benefit from distinguishing an exploratory argument or draft from text whose evidence is ready for an independent check. The distinction already has a precedent in the provisional introduction. Two smaller mismatches matter at the point of use. The README’s diagram puts author decisions after the re-check, while `revise` calls for the relevant decisions before edits. The README and drafting inputs also present the style sheet as expected, while its technique expressly allows plain register. `skills/paper/start.md:34–46`, `skills/paper/draft.md:5–15`, `skills/paper/draft.md:44–53`, `skills/paper/sections.md:47–55`, `README.md:34–55`, `skills/paper/revise.md:17–28`, `skills/paper/techniques/style-sheet.md:1–5`

Core mixes portable duties with paper-specific mechanics. Its distinctions between primary evidence, reader judgement and author decision travel well. Its LaTeX forms, `STATE.md`, commit policy and manuscript rules belong to paper work. The literature skill already demonstrates the better boundary through its optional notebook and conversation-only output. The outside-workspace reader protocol demonstrates it through caller-supplied inputs and pins. Make those boundaries discoverable before asking a caller to absorb the paper conventions. `skills/_shared/core.md:3–18`, `skills/_shared/core.md:20–70`, `skills/literature/SKILL.md:64–92`, `skills/paper/review.md:16–27`

### L4 — Reader briefs and techniques

### L5 — Tools and scaffold

The scaffold is small and legible. It separates current state, argument, claim verdicts, open questions and decision history. The default article builds toward ordinary LaTeX, with placeholders for framing and declarations. The initial routing has a small ambiguity: `start` is entered when there is no `STATE.md`, but copying the scaffold creates a `STATE.md` that still tells the caller to run `start`. Distinguish an uninitialised scaffold from a paper being resumed. The source policy also invites a practical correction: directory-only exclusions cannot express the test workspace’s convention excluding one forward-plans file. `skeleton/paper-workspace/paper/STATE.md:3–23`, `skeleton/paper-workspace/paper/outline.md:1–23`, `skeleton/paper-workspace/paper/claims.md:1–7`, `skeleton/paper-workspace/paper/open-questions.md:1–5`, `skeleton/paper-workspace/paper/journal.md:1–3`, `skeleton/paper-workspace/manuscript/main.tex:11–50`, `skills/paper/start.md:3–14`, `tools/traces.py:372–402`, `ai-agent-science-paper-workspace/AGENTS.md:16–28`

The trace tool’s strongest feature is its narrow remit. `lost` matches normalised trace text, `stale` dates a manuscript file relative to a committed stamp, and `moved` looks for path anchors in changed tracked sources. These checks direct attention. They leave sentence support, missing traces, the scientific effect of a changed result and the legitimacy of a removed gap to review. Be especially precise about `lost` calling a removed marker “gap closed.” That output describes a textual deletion, whose scientific disposition still needs a reason. `tools/traces.py:21–24`, `tools/traces.py:46–68`, `tools/traces.py:319–364`, `tools/traces.py:634–666`, `skills/paper/revise.md:17–28`

## SECTION: PART2_CRITIQUE_OF_REFLECTION

The reflection is strongest where it identifies concrete friction and traces it to a procedure. Its recommendation to build around the owner’s delegation generalises a deliberately unusual arrangement. “Roughly three traced sentences in ten” also overstates what the count measures. The journal distinguishes traces, anchors, findings, changes and missed coverage. Several findings can concern one sentence, and one finding can affect several. The counts establish that review found consequential problems, while reader recall, error prevalence and the proposed savings remain unmeasured. The reflection itself documents the unusual case and limited model variation. `docs/reflection-2026-11/REFLECTION.md:9–23`, `docs/reflection-2026-11/REFLECTION.md:37–51`, `docs/reflection-2026-11/REFLECTION.md:60–104`, `docs/reflection-2026-11/REFLECTION.md:336–343`, `ai-agent-science-paper-workspace/paper/journal.md:62–71`, `ai-agent-science-paper-workspace/paper/journal.md:85–88`
