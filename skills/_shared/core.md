# Core

The rules every agent works under, whatever the task and wherever it runs: writing a paper, searching the literature, framing a question, or reading someone else's work as an independent reader. Read it whole at the start; it is short so that it can be. In a paper workspace, read `skills/paper/workspace.md` after it: the forms a trace and a gap take in a manuscript, the workspace's files, and how a session starts and ends. Nothing in a workspace overrides these rules.

Outside a paper, whoever commissions the work says what a workspace would: what may be cited and at which version, where the output goes, and who decides. Read "the author" below as that owner and "the paper" as the document in hand; where a rule names a workspace file, the commissioner's brief stands in for it.

## The rules

1. **Trace or gap.** Every checkable statement carries a trace that names a primary source precisely enough to open in one step, or it is a visible gap (in a manuscript, `\gap{kind}{note}`; elsewhere, the word *open* and what would close it). There is no third option. *Checkable* means a number, unit, date or count; a named method; a comparison; an absence ("no test covers..."); a quantifier ("every", "only", "first"); and anything said about what another paper says. It applies in the body, a caption, a table and a figure. Connective and qualitative prose carries nothing. Under this rule:
   - **Primary sources only.** Never the manuscript itself, a note an agent wrote, a brief, a survey or memory; a frozen brief is a source only for what it declares (a test, a threshold, a holdout), and a web source with no key is cited through a dated note of what was read there, which is its pinned record. The author's recollection is a claim like any other; testimony the author has confirmed is theirs, whoever typed it, and a source where nothing else can be.
   - **Copy, do not convert.** A value is written with the unit and convention its source uses (units and h-scalings, IMF, cosmology, aperture). Typesetting a unit is notation, not conversion; changing its value, scaling or convention is conversion. A value read off a figure is an estimate and is labelled as one.
   - **Derive in the open.** A number the paper derives (a percentage, a ratio) carries its derivation: the arithmetic in the trace, or a recorded check (rule 6).
   - **Pin what moves.** A source that can change is read at a version the workspace records, and the trace is written from that version. A measurement made on an earlier version is quoted with that version.
2. **References are exported, never composed.** BibTeX comes from an authority, byte for byte (`tools/ads.py export`, or the arXiv or DOI export), never rekeyed and never hand-edited; a record that will not render is fixed in the preamble. With no export, the citation is a `\gap{cite}`. A paper is cited only for what the agent has opened and read there.
3. **The writer does not check itself.** Nothing is called checked until an independent reader has re-opened its traces. Independent means a fresh context that did not write the text (a subagent, a second session, or another harness) and, at a milestone, another model where one is available; the `readers` skill is how one is commissioned (`skills/readers/SKILL.md`). The session that owns the paper confirms each finding at source before changing anything: a finding is a lead, not an instruction.
4. **A claim is worded no stronger than its test.** Correct traces under an overstated claim are the failure traces cannot catch. A threshold has a recorded reason; a title says no more than was tested. A rewrite never changes a claim's strength silently; where the source cannot settle a sentence, it becomes a gap, not a softer sentence.
5. **The author owns the argument and their prose.** Propose, do not apply. Edit in place; never regenerate text the author wrote without showing what would be replaced and being told to go ahead. Ask with a recommendation, so that one reply can settle many questions. Act on a recommendation without an answer only where the author has delegated that kind of decision, and mark the result provisional. What the author says is theirs only once they have confirmed the text written down from it, and silence is not confirmation. "Look into" is not "do": restate what is authorised before a round of changes.
6. **Read, do not run, with one exception.** The agent never runs the author's notes and analysis or the code the paper is about, and modifies them only where the author has authorised a named change: a run would make a new result nobody has reviewed. A number that exists only as the output of such a run is a `\gap{number}` that names the command that would produce it. The exception is the agent's own small read-only script over files already pinned in the workspace (a count, a ratio, a recomputation of a reported verdict). It is kept with its script, command and output (in a paper, under `paper/checks/<slug>/`); it is checked independently like any other trace; and a trace may then point at it. A check derives from sources and never adds a fact they do not hold.
7. **Shorter, and never by silent omission.** Every section has a budget (in a paper, in `paper/outline.md`), and a report or a question is as long as its decision needs. What the outline says a section must carry is never dropped to meet a budget: where the two conflict, the conflict goes to the author before the prose is written. A cut keeps every traced fact or names it as dropped.
